# Bellabeat Case Study — Turning Sleep into the Gateway to Retention

End-to-end marketing data analysis (Google Data Analytics Capstone),
from business question to executive report.

How do smart wellness device users actually behave? One month of
tracker data (33 users, 4.8M raw records) reveals four distinct
user segments, one early marker of churn, and three sourced
marketing recommendations for a women's wellness brand.

## Key findings
- Only 30% of users adopt sleep tracking — its abandonment
  predicts total disengagement (-35% activity in 3 weeks).
- Four personas emerge (completes, engaged day-only, inconsistent,
  decayers): one marketing message cannot speak to all four.
- Reality sits far below industry benchmarks (10,000 steps,
  7h sleep) for most user-days — the gap IS the positioning.
- Two natural daily contact windows: lunch (12-14) and evening
  wind-down (20-22).

## Method
Ask → Prepare → Process → Analyze → Share (Google Data Analytics
cycle). Python (pandas, matplotlib) in a Kaggle notebook.

Discipline: findings are facts from a 33-user 2016 sample; market
claims are sourced separately; every recommendation carries its
stated limit. The dataset cannot describe women or today's market —
it identifies durable behavioral structures only.

## Data
FitBit Fitness Tracker Data (Möbius, Kaggle, CC0 Public Domain).
33 users verified (vs 30 announced), 2016-04-12 to 2016-05-12.
