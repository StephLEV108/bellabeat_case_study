# Phase 1: Ask — Bellabeat Case Study

## 1. Core Business Question
How do women who use smart health devices actually behave — and how
can those behaviors guide Bellabeat's marketing strategy for its app
and connected products, to convert general wellness consumers into
engaged Bellabeat platform users?

Guiding questions from Urška Sršen (CCO):
1. What usage behaviors dominate among smart health device users?
2. How do those behaviors apply to Bellabeat's target consumers —
   women focused on holistic well-being?
3. How can these insights be translated into concrete marketing
   opportunities for Bellabeat?

## 2. Scope and Focus Product
The analysis will be applied primarily to the Bellabeat app, with the
Leaf as secondary flagship product. The app is the convergence point of
all metrics (activity, sleep, stress, cycle) — and the point where
conversion to subscription happens.

## 3. Data Judgment — Statement of Limitations
The available data (FitBit Fitness Tracker Data, Kaggle, 2016) has
serious constraints, declared here deliberately before analysis begins:

- Age: 2016 — no conclusion about current market trends can be drawn
  from it directly.
- Sample: 30 users — not representative.
- Demographics: no documented gender — extrapolation to Bellabeat's
  female customer base is a working hypothesis, not an established
  fact.
- Duration: 31 days — a behavioral snapshot, not a longitudinal study.

Methodological decision: these data will NOT be used to describe the
market. They will be used only to identify DURABLE BEHAVIORAL
STRUCTURES (user subgroups, device-wearing patterns, engagement
intensity). Such structures change slowly because they are rooted in
human nature, not technology. Every recommendation derived from the
2016 data will then be confronted with the 2025-2026 market context
below, and framed as a hypothesis to validate — not a certainty.

## 4. Market Context 2025-2026 (external interpretation layer)
Four structural evolutions of the female wearables market frame the
reading of insights:

1. Sleep has become a central health pillar (sleep tracking market
   projected to reach ~$7.9B by 2032, CAGR 15.5%) — sleep quality
   now outranks simple hour-counting.
2. Migration toward discreet design (smart rings, thin bracelets,
   pendants) at the expense of bulky watches — a structural advantage
   for Leaf and Time.
3. Medical-grade fertility precision (fertility tracker market
   projected to roughly double by 2032) — real physiological
   measurement replacing approximate statistical estimates.
4. Demand for a unified holistic view where AI correlates metrics
   (cycle, sleep, stress, activity) instead of fragmenting them.

Role of this layer: it translates behaviors observed in 2016 into
recommendations relevant for 2026. It will never be presented as
validated by the analyzed data. All market figures to be sourced and
cited in the Share phase.

## 5. Stakeholders
- Urška Sršen (CCO): seeking growth and differentiation opportunities.
- Sando Mur (co-founder, mathematician): will demand methodological
  rigor — which is why data limitations are declared at this stage.
- Marketing Analytics team: will turn recommendations into campaigns.
- Executive team: will decide budget allocation.

## 6. Success Criteria
- At least 3 distinct behavioral insights, each backed by a metric
  from the data.
- Each insight converted into one recommendation, with its
  methodological limit made explicit.
- Deliverable: a report with visualizations, written for a
  non-technical reader, readable in 10 minutes.

## 7. What This Analysis Does NOT Claim to Do
- Establish current market shares.
- Describe Bellabeat's actual customers (no demographic data available).
- Demonstrate causality (observational data only).
- Read users' motivations or intentions — the data contains behavior,
  not psychology.
