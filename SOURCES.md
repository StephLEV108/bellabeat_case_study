# Sources — Bellabeat Case Study, Share Phase

## Reading guide
Every market figure used in the Share report is listed here with its
origin and date. Where a figure could not be verified against its
primary source, the report says so instead of citing it.

## Sleep market (Hypothesis 1 — retention via sleep)
- Data Bridge Market Research (2024): sleep tracking market valued
  ~USD 26.6B in 2024.
- Verified Market Research (2024): sleep tracker market projected to
  reach USD 68.3B by 2032, CAGR 12.4%.
- Grand View Research (2024): sleep tracker market projected to
  reach USD 58.2B by 2030.
  Note: an earlier figure of USD 7.9B by 2032, present in early
  drafts, was found obsolete and corrected before the Share report.
  The market is large and growing at double-digit rates — not
  emerging, but mainstream.
- Trend synthesis: sleep quality now outranks hour-counting as the
  consumer expectation of sleep tracking.

## Fertility tracking (Hypothesis 1 — precision)
- Industry analyses (2025, market research syntheses): fertility and
  pregnancy tracking market projected to grow at ~13.2% CAGR through
  2032 — more than doubling over the period. Absolute market sizes
  vary widely across firms; the trajectory, not the level, is cited.
- WHO: PCOS affects an estimated 10-13% of women worldwide, with up
  to 70% of cases undiagnosed.
- Insee (France, 2023): mean age at childbirth 31.0 years; mean age
  at first child 29.1 years, rising continuously.
- Eurostat (2023): mean age of women at first child ~29-31 years
  across EU countries.

## Perimenopause (Hypothesis 2 — the unserved segment)
- FemTech trend analyses (2026): ~1 billion women projected to be in
  perimenopause or menopause by 2030.
- Clue (cycle-tracking app, published survey data): ~70% of women
  do not know what perimenopause is — the educational gap is the
  opportunity.
- FemTech market growth: ~13.6% CAGR projected 2025-2032 (market
  research syntheses).

## Discreet design migration (Hypothesis 2/3 — product direction)
- IDC shipment data (2025, relayed by Bloomberg): smart ring
  shipments up 49% in 2025, vs 6% for smartwatches.
- Omdia (H1 2025): Oura holds 74% of the smart ring market —
  discretion wins.
- Synthesis: bulky wristbands are structurally declining; jewelry-
  form trackers (pendant, bracelet, ring) align with the trend.

## Quantified-self anxiety (Hypothesis 3 — anti-perfectionism)
- Systematic reviews of wearables and mental health (NIH/PMC
  indexed literature): a documented tension between self-tracking
  and anxiety/stress for a subset of users. Cited in the report as
  "a documented debate," not as a percentage — the literature does
  not support a single number.
